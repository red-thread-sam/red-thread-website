Creativity and performance shouldn't live in separate silos.

We built Red Thread to put creators at the undisputed core of the media plan – exactly where they belong.

Red Thread was born out of a shared frustration, but it also started from a deeply personal place.

For years, we watched global brands treat influencer marketing as a chaotic, last-minute add-on. It never made sense to us because we have spent our careers living on both sides of the divide. We know exactly what it feels like to be the creator trying to protect an audience's trust, and we know what it takes to sit in the boardroom delivering predictable ROI for a Fortune 500 brand.

Our team is one of the few that has genuinely lived both realities, meaning native creator instinct isn't just a strategic buzzword for us – it is woven directly into our DNA.

Consumer behaviour is overwhelmingly driven by digital subcultures, yet the creator economy is still often treated as a below-the-line afterthought. We knew the industry needed a completely new model that finally brings the commercial rigour of enterprise media planning to the raw, human intuition of the creator space.

So we built it.

Because we don't hold restrictive talent rosters, our approach remains entirely objective. By combining our proprietary Attention Engine™ with this lived creator instinct and absolute operational precision, we've built a new ecosystem for brands and creators to collaborate effortlessly.

We care deeply about this space and the people in it. We aren't here to guess, but rather to turn feed culture into measurable commercial impact – and we are already proving it alongside forward-thinking partners like Movember and Gamma.
